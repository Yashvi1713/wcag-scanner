How this was tested

  1. Automated rules. The axe-core {{ scan.axe_version }} engine ran on every page at desktop (1280px) and phone (375px) sizes. It covers {{ scan.axe_coverage|length }} of the criteria at this level.
  2. Interaction tests. A scripted keyboard user pressed Tab through each page to find keyboard traps, missing focus indicators, hidden or obscured focus and controls the keyboard can't reach. The page was also tested at 200% zoom, with increased text spacing and at 320px wide (reflow), and checked for autoplaying media and endless animation.
  3. Cross-page tests. Pages were compared for unique titles, consistent navigation, consistent link names, consistent help and more than one way to find pages.
  4. AI review. {% if scan.ai_enabled %}Claude reviewed screenshots and content on up to 5 pages for meaningful alt text, clear link text, descriptive headings and use of colour. These findings are marked with a confidence level and should be confirmed by a person.{% else %}Not used for this scan.{% endif %}
  5. Manual checks. Criteria that no test covers are listed above for a person to check.

Limitations

Automated testing finds roughly a third to a half of accessibility issues. "Pass" means no problems were detected, not that the criterion is met. Content behind logins, forms after submission, and states that need interaction (menus, dialogs, errors) were not tested.
